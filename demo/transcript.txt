$ adrgate explain D1
D1 REVERSAL - the record states reversal conditions

What it checks: The record must state the conditions under which the decision would be reversed, in its own words (for example: 'We reverse this decision if latency exceeds 200 ms for 7 consecutive days'). A bare mention of the word 'reverse' is not enough; a condition (if / when / should / unless) must be present.

Why it matters: Without a reversal condition, a reversal looks arbitrary and nobody can say what changed. The decision can be undone silently, and the next team inherits a choice with no tripwire.

Anchor: On 18/11/2026 Nvidia's board said it had no intention of reinstating Jensen Huang as CEO; on 22/11/2026 it voted unanimously to bring him back, and on 23/11/2026 Nvidia announced the reversal. No published reversal condition explains what changed in five days.

Threshold: Pass: a reversal-condition sentence is present. Fail: no reversal language, or reversal mentioned without a condition.

Source: Nvidia timeline as reported 23/11/2026 (Morningstar/Dow Jones, TechCrunch, Bloomberg Law, CoinDesk).
